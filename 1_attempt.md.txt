### Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 97 rows and 6 columns. It includes weekly prices for four products across two cities, covering January to March 2026.

The columns are:

- Item
- City
- Price
- Unit
- Data Label

### 2. Problems Identified

1. **Missing Value:** One price is missing for Pulse C in City 2 on 1 January 2026.

2. **Incorrect Formatting:** One price is written as 1,250, which needs to be checked and converted into a numeric format.

3. **Unusual Price:** A price of 9999 appears for Sweetener D in City 1. This needs investigation.

4. **Inconsistent Product Name:** One record uses "oil b" instead of "Oil B".

5. **Duplicate Record:** The record for Staple A, City 1, dated 8 January 2026 appears twice.

6. **Data Types:** The date and price columns are currently stored as text.

### 3. Planned Cleaning Decisions

| Problem | Planned decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Missing price | Investigate and flag | Avoid guessing a price |
| Price 1,250 | Convert and investigate | Check numeric formatting |
| Price 9999 | Flag for verification | Could be an error |
| oil b | Standardize to Oil B if confirmed | Consistent naming |
| Duplicate | Remove extra copy after verification | Avoid double counting |
| Date format | Convert to datetime | Improve analysis |
| Price format | Convert to numeric | Allow calculations |

### 4. Initial Conclusion

The dataset contains formatting issues, missing information, a duplicate record and unusual values. These problems should be investigated before making changes.
